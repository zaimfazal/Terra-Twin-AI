# General background only

Jezero Crater is a Martian impact crater that preserves evidence of an ancient lake and a river delta. NASA's Mars 2020 Perseverance rover landed in Jezero Crater to investigate its geology, ancient environmental conditions and potential past habitability.

The reference target's scientific interests in the platform catalogue are sedimentary geology, ancient water-related processes, delta formations, mineralogical characteristics and environmental history.
