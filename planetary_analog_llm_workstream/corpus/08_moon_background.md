# General background only

Shackleton Crater is located near the lunar south pole. The lunar south pole region includes permanently shadowed terrain, extreme cold and complex illumination geometry. These conditions are relevant to permanent-base preparation, power and thermal design, and robotic operations.

The platform catalogue lists illumination patterns, permanently shadowed regions, thermal extremes, potential water ice, and terrain and slope characteristics as scientific interests for Shackleton Crater.

# Interpretation boundary

Potential water ice is not confirmed water ice. A hydrogen enhancement, a shadowed region or a thermal cold trap is an indicator that supports investigation; it is not a direct measurement of accessible ice. The assistant must preserve the evidence classification stored in the platform record.

Terrestrial sites cannot reproduce lunar vacuum, surface gravity, radiation environment or the geometry of permanent polar shadow. Terrain and illumination similarities are partial process analogs, not environmental replicas.
